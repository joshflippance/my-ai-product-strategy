# The Prototype Bet

## What I Built
Season Draft: a one-page AI feature for Cottage Book that drafts a co-owned cottage's whole summer from each family branch's wish list and share. It checks every week against each branch's hard limits in code, tags each week as clear, close call or contested, explains every clash in nights, and writes the note to send the family.

## Tool Used
Other (Claude, published as a Claude artifact)

## Prototype Link
https://claude.ai/artifact/W67a6fskoYcuErZ2vFydV5

## AI Value Archetype
Oracle

## The Bet in One Sentence
Families who co-own a cottage will accept an AI-drafted summer if it shows every branch's fair share in nights and explains each clash, and now that Cottage Book already tracks branches and shares, turning a weeks-long group chat negotiation into one draft is worth paying for.

## Kill Criteria
Kill it if, in tests with 5 co-owned cottages using their real wish lists, fewer than 3 adopt the draft with 2 or fewer week changes, or if any family reports a draft that broke a stated hard limit without the guardrail catching it.
