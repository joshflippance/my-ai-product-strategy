# The Prototype Bet

## What I Built
Tradeoff Room: a one-page AI decision assistant that ranks roadmap requests against fixed engineering capacity, draws the cut line, explains every cut in numbers, and drafts a Now / Next / Later view plus a stakeholder summary ready to send.

## Tool Used
Other (Claude, published as a Claude artifact)

## Prototype Link
https://claude.ai/artifact/Dz7QF1zq8BeuTVupMDEBrH

## AI Value Archetype
Oracle

## The Bet in One Sentence
Directors of Product facing more requests than capacity will trust AI to draw the quarterly cut line if it shows its reasoning in numbers, and now that every roadmap review is a tradeoff fight, cutting prep from days to under an hour is worth paying for.

## Kill Criteria
Kill it if, in tests with 5 product leaders using their own roadmap, fewer than 3 accept the recommended cut line with 2 or fewer changes, or fewer than 3 would send the generated stakeholder summary after light edits.
